What navigatr does

Two audiences, one system. As an admin, you onboard your team, set who reports to whom, and watch the pipeline fill. Your reps live on their phones between drop-ins, and navigatr routes their day so every stop has a reason.

For admins

Onboard and oversee

Create a workspace, invite your reps and managers, set reporting lines, and follow activity, pipeline, and wins as the team works.

For reps

Work the field

See businesses nearby, run a smart driving route, drop in, log the outcome in a tap or two, and let follow-ups turn into deals automatically.

Part 1 · Admin

Set up your workspace

Do this once, at app.getnavigatr.io, on a laptop. The first person to create the workspace becomes its administrator.

1

Create your workspace

Choose Create account and sign up with your work email (or continue with Google). Leave the optional Invite code field blank — that's what tells navigatr you're starting a new company workspace. Agree to the Terms, then name your workspace (usually your company name; it shows in the top bar).

2

Invite your team

Right after setup you land on the invite step. Later, invite anytime from the Team tab. Two ways in:

  • One at a time — Invite agent: enter their email, pick a role level, and optionally who they report to.
  • A whole roster — Import CSV: upload a spreadsheet with columns email, full_name, role_level, and reports_to_email. A sample template is on the import screen. You can list a manager and their reps in the same file — navigatr links them up as everyone accepts.

Only an Administrator or CSO / CRO can send invites.

3

Your reps accept

Each person gets an email invitation. They open it, see who invited them and their role, set a password, and they're in — already attached to your workspace and their manager. No setup on their end.

Part 1 · Admin

Roles & the org chart

Roles decide what a person can see and do; reporting lines decide whose numbers roll up to whom. Both are set on the Team tab — toggle between List and Org chart to see the structure.

The seven role levels

From the top down

Administrator · CSO / CRO · SVP of Sales · VP of Sales · Director of Sales · Sales Manager · Sales Professional. A manager sees their own team's work; a rep sees their own.

Changing things later

One person at a time

Open anyone from the Team list to change their Role level or who they Report to. Role level is administrator-only. A person's role is set once they've accepted their invite.

Part 2 · Reps

Run your day

This is the loop a rep lives in, built for a phone in a moving car: open Path, and navigatr proposes today's route. Every stop tells you why it's on the list.

01

Open Path → "Your day"

navigatr builds a route from your calendar, your promises, and who's overdue for a visit. Tap start and go — or explore what's nearby first.

02

See who's nearby

Inside Path, browse real businesses around you. Filter by category, set a radius, and sort by Popular, Nearest, or Opportunity. These are prospects near you, not yet stops on your day.

03

Run the route

Each stop shows a plain reason — appointment, you promised, anytime, or on the way — and three buttons only: I'm here, Navigate, Skip for now. No clutter while you're driving.

04

Log the stop

Tap I'm here, pick the outcome that fits, add a note by typing or by voice (Dictate), and hit Log Stop. Two taps and you're back on the road.

05

Follow-ups become deals

Choose an outcome like They asked me back and navigatr sets a follow-up date and starts a deal in your pipeline — so nothing you promised falls through the cracks.

Part 2 · Reps

Pipeline & calendar

Pipeline

Every deal, one board

The Pipeline tab tracks each deal through New → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation → Won / Lost. Start one anytime with Add Deal; drops that ask you back open a deal for you.

Calendar

Meetings on your route

Connect Google or Outlook in Settings → Integrations. Your appointments show up as stops on Path at the right time, and navigatr adds the follow-ups and appointments you book to your calendar.

Good to know during the beta

What's live right now connected

Business discovery and routing use real maps data, calendar connections are real, and voice-to-text on notes works out of the box. You're using the real thing, not a demo.

  • Help is one tap away. Use the in-app messenger (bottom corner) to ask a question or flag anything rough — that feedback is exactly what the beta is for.
  • Mobile-first for reps, laptop for setup. Reps can run their whole day on a phone; do the workspace setup and CSV import on a computer.
  • We're still polishing. Onboarding and reporting are improving week over week. If something feels unfinished, tell us — we're listening closely during the beta.
